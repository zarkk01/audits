# Findings

# [M-01] All UsdcOrders will be swapped unfairly for depositors upon CBR.

## Description
When a user wants to exchange his USD0 for USYC, he must call redeem of DaoCollateral function and burn the stablecoins to get back his collateral tokens. In this process, there is a redemption fee and as stated in docs users are supposed to query redeemFee function so to see how many percent fee they will pay. However, when CBR is on and users don't redeem their USD0 1:1 with USYC but less, the fee is applied to the original amount. This increases the redempion fee unfairly for the users who already got their funds cut and also do not cohere with the redeemFee() that is supposed to be return the fee percent. Also, in these cases users can pay lot more than 25%(which is the maximum fee percent) of their USD0 as a fee.

## Proof of Concept
To understand this vulnerability better, consider this scenario :

Alice swap 200000 USYC and gets 180000 USD0.
For some reason, CBR is activated on DaoCollateral and the coeff becomes 0.6.
Alice calls redeemFee and sees that the redemption fee is 10%.
Alice calls redeem expecting to get 60% of her collateral token back and pay 10% fee. However, Alice pays 18000 USD0 as redemption fee and gets back USYC that would be equivelant to 0.6 * (180000e18 - 18000e18) = 97200 USD0.
So to wrap it up. Alice got back USYC which is worth 97200 USD0 and payed 18000 USD0 fee. This is 18.5% fee while Alice waited and would fairly be charged 10%.

## Severity
The likelihood of this happening seems to be medium because in order to be CBR on should USYC to lose value which is not impossible since it is a new ERC20 and not so much tested in time and in production. The impact that this vulnerability has is also medium because users pay more fees than it should but the funds are not lost, the protocol just overprofits. To conclude, we believe that this finding is of medium severity due to the above reasons

## Recommendation
Consider charging the fee after the calculation of the returnedCollateral in _burnStableTokenAndTransferCollateral for a fair redemption.
